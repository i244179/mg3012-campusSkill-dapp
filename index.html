Hi, learner

Turn what you learn into a permanent record.

CampusSkill lets you publish personal learning milestones from your own wallet to the Ethereum Sepolia test network. Every milestone keeps its creator, its timestamps and its completion status on-chain, so anyone can check your progress.

How CampusSkill works

  1. Connect MetaMask on the Sepolia network
  2. Publish a milestone with a title and description
  3. Mark it as completed when you have finished it
